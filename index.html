PlyCount

An independent iPhone app for Ravelry.

PlyCount lets you search patterns, yarns, designers, people and projects, keep your library, stash, queue, favorites and projects up to date, read your pattern PDFs, save your searches, take part in Ravelry's forums and see your friends' recent activity, all with your own Ravelry account.

The app is free, has no ads, and does not track you.

Get help · Read the privacy policy

PlyCount is an independent app. It is not made, provided, endorsed or affiliated with Ravelry, and is a separate service from Ravelry. Ravelry has its own terms and privacy policy.

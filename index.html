PlyCount

An independent iPhone app for Ravelry, by Jamie Tippett.

PlyCount lets you search patterns, yarns and projects, keep your library, stash, queue and projects up to date, read your pattern PDFs, and save your searches, all with your own Ravelry account.

The app is free, has no ads, and does not track you. If you want to help cover its running costs, there is an optional tip in the app.

PlyCount is an independent app and is a separate service from Ravelry. Ravelry has its own terms and privacy policy.

A Ravelry companion for your iPhone

Search patterns and yarns, keep your library, stash and queue up to date, and read your pattern PDFs. All with your own Ravelry account.

Coming soon to the App Store

Free, with no ads and no tracking. For iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

    PlyCount comes in six colors. Pick one.

    What it does

    Search Ravelry
    Search patterns, yarns, designers, people and projects.
    Library and stash
    Keep your library and your stash up to date from your phone.
    Queue, favorites and projects
    Add to your queue, save favorites, and update your projects while you work.
    Pattern PDFs
    Read your pattern PDFs in the app. Your notes and highlights stay on your device.
    Saved searches
    Save a search once and run it again whenever you like.
    Forums and friends
    Take part in Ravelry's forums and see what your friends have been up to.
    Yarn shops near you
    Find local yarn shops, using your location only while the app is open and only if you ask.
    Yours to set up
    Light, dark or automatic appearance, and six colors for the app and its icon.

    PlyCount is independent of Ravelry

    PlyCount is an independent app. It is not made, provided, endorsed or affiliated with Ravelry, and is a separate service from Ravelry. Ravelry has its own terms and privacy policy.

    You sign in on Ravelry's own page, so PlyCount never sees your Ravelry password. PlyCount runs no server that receives your data, and it does not track you. Read the privacy policy.

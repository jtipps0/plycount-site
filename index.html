PlyCount

A Ravelry companion for your iPhone

Search Ravelry, keep your library, stash and queue up to date, and read your pattern PDFs. All with your own Ravelry account.

What it does

Search Ravelry
Search patterns, yarns, designers, people, projects and more, with Ravelry's own filters.
Queue, favorites and projects
Add to your queue, save favorites, and update your projects while you work.
Library and stash
Keep your library, stash and handspun up to date from your phone.
Project and stash photos
Add photos to a project or a stash entry from your photo library or the camera. Drag to reorder them, pick a cover photo, or delete one.
Pattern PDFs
Read your Ravelry PDFs in the app, or add your own. Make notes and highlights just for you.
Available offline
Download a pattern PDF and open it with no signal. Your Projects, Favorites and Library lists still show the patterns you've downloaded.
Saved searches
Save a search once and run it again whenever you like. Import your saved searches from Ravelry.
Forums and friends
Take part in Ravelry's forums and see what your friends have been up to.
Yarn shops near you
Find local yarn shops, using your location only while the app is open and only if you ask.
Yours to set up
Light, dark or automatic appearance, and six colors for the app and its icon. Put the features you use the most in the bottom bar for easy access.

PlyCount is independent of Ravelry

PlyCount is an independent app. It is not made, provided, endorsed or affiliated with Ravelry, and is a separate service from Ravelry. Ravelry has its own terms and privacy policy.

You sign in on Ravelry's own page, so PlyCount never sees your Ravelry password. PlyCount runs no server that receives your data, and it does not track you. Read the privacy policy.
